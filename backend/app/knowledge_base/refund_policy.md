# Return and Refund Policy

## Overview
We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied, we offer a straightforward return and refund policy.

## Eligibility Window
- Customers are eligible to request a return or full refund within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and with all original tags attached.

## Process
1. Contact support with your **Order Number** and reason for return.
2. A prepaid return shipping label will be generated and emailed to your registered address.
3. Once the returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse, your refund will be processed within **5–7 business days**.
4. Refunds are credited back to the **original payment method** used during checkout.

## Non-Refundable Items
- Digital gift cards, downloadable software products, and personalized/custom-made items are non-refundable.

# Payment Methods and Card Declines

## Accepted Payment Methods
We support a variety of secure payment methods:
- **Credit / Debit Cards:** Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.
- **Digital Wallets:** Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.
- **Local Bank Transfers:** Available for enterprise invoice orders.

## Common Reasons for Card Declines
If your payment failed during checkout:
1. **Incorrect Billing Zip/Postal Code:** Ensure the billing address matches the address on file with your card-issuing bank.
2. **3D Secure / OTP Failure:** Make sure your mobile phone receives the 2FA SMS code or banking app push confirmation.
3. **Daily Transaction Limits:** Check with your bank if an online transaction limit is blocking the charge.
4. **Fraud Protection Blocks:** Foreign or unusual transaction flags may prompt your bank to pause authorization until verified.

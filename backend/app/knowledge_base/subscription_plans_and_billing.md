# Subscription Plans and Billing Management

## Billing Cycles
- Subscriptions are billed automatically on a recurring **monthly** or **annual** basis depending on the chosen tier.
- Invoices and billing receipts are emailed automatically on the first day of each billing cycle.

## Upgrading and Downgrading
- You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time in **Settings > Subscription**.
- Upgrades take effect immediately with pro-rated charges for the remainder of the billing period.
- Downgrades take effect at the beginning of the next billing cycle.

## Cancelling a Subscription
To prevent auto-renewal, cancel your subscription at least **24 hours** before your next billing date. You will retain full access until the end of your current paid billing period.

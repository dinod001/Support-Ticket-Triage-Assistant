# Account Deletion and Privacy Rights

## Permanent Account Closure
We respect your privacy and right to be forgotten (GDPR / CCPA compliance). You can request account deletion at any time.

## Self-Service Deletion
1. Sign in to your account and go to **Settings > Privacy & Security**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and select **"Delete My Account"**.
3. Confirm by re-entering your account password and reviewing the deletion notice.

## 14-Day Grace Period
- After initiating deletion, your account enters a **14-day grace period**. During this period, your profile and data are hidden from public view.
- If you change your mind, simply log in within the 14 days and click **"Cancel Deletion"** to restore your account.
- After 14 days, all personal identifiers, order history data, and payment records are permanently and irreversibly purged from our database.

# Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation Window
Because our automated fulfillment warehouse processes orders quickly to ensure rapid delivery:
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge within **1 hour** of placement.
- If the order status has already progressed to **"Dispatched"** or **"In Transit"**, it can no longer be cancelled.

## How to Cancel
1. Go to **My Orders** in your account dashboard.
2. If your order is still in **"Pending"** or **"Processing"** status, click the **"Cancel Order"** button.
3. You will receive an instant email confirmation, and a full refund will be processed immediately.

## What if the Order Has Already Shipped?
If the cancellation window has passed, you can refuse delivery upon arrival with the carrier or initiate a return within 30 days under our standard Return Policy.

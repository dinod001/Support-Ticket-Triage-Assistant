# Password Reset and Account Security

## Resetting Your Password
If you have forgotten your password or need to reset your account credentials:
1. Navigate to the login page and click on the **"Forgot Password?"** link.
2. Enter your registered account email address.
3. An automated password reset link will be dispatched to your inbox within 2 minutes.
4. For security reasons, the reset link is valid for **15 minutes** from generation.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Recovery
- If you have lost access to your authenticator app (TOTP), use one of the emergency backup codes provided during initial setup.
- If backup codes are unavailable, contact security support directly with your registered email and proof of identification for manual verification.

## Account Lockout
For security protection, accounts are temporarily locked for **30 minutes** after 5 consecutive incorrect password attempts.

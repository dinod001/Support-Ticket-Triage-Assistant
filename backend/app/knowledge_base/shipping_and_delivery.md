# Shipping and Delivery Information

## Delivery Options and Timelines
We offer both standard and express shipping options across all domestic regions:
- **Standard Shipping:** Delivers within **3–5 business days**. Free on orders over $50.
- **Express Shipping:** Delivers within **1–2 business days**. Flat rate fee of $12.99.

## Order Tracking
- Once your order has been packed and handed over to our courier partner, you will receive an automated shipping confirmation email containing your **Tracking Number** and a tracking link.
- Tracking status may take up to **24 hours** to reflect real-time updates after courier pickup.

## Delivery Delays
In the rare event of severe weather conditions, holidays, or carrier disruptions, deliveries might experience delays of 1–2 additional days. If your order has not arrived within 7 business days, please reach out to customer support with your tracking ID.

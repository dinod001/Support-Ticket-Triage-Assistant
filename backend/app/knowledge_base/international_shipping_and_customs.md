# International Shipping, Duties and Customs

## International Shipping Timelines
- Standard International Shipping delivers within **7–14 business days** depending on the destination country and customs clearance.
- Express International Shipping delivers within **3–5 business days** via DHL Express or FedEx.

## Customs, Import Duties & VAT
- Orders shipped outside our primary distribution jurisdiction may be subject to import taxes, customs duties, and import fees levied by the destination country.
- Unless explicitly indicated as "DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)" at checkout, the recipient is the importer of record and is responsible for all customs clearance fees.

## Tracking International Orders
International shipments are tracked end-to-end. Once the package clears destination customs, local postal services take over final mile delivery using the same tracking identifier.

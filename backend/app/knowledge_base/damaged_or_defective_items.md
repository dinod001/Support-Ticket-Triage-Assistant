# Damaged or Defective Items Policy

## Immediate Assistance for Damaged Deliveries
If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, we will replace it or issue a full refund immediately at no extra cost to you.

## Reporting Window & Requirements
- Damaged shipments must be reported within **48 hours** of carrier delivery.
- Please provide your **Order Number** and clear photographs showing:
  1. The damaged product itself.
  2. The exterior shipping package showing the shipping label.

## Resolution Options
Upon verifying the provided photographs, the customer support team will offer two options:
1. **Free Immediate Expedited Replacement:** We dispatch a brand new unit via next-day courier.
2. **Full Refund:** We immediately refund 100% of the purchase amount including original shipping charges.

You will generally not be required to ship back the damaged product unless requested by our quality assurance team.
